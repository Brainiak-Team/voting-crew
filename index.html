    Governor: Vivek Ramaswamy (R) vs. Amy Acton (D)

    IssueVivek Ramaswamy (R)Amy Acton (D)

    Abortion / life

    Vivek Ramaswamy (R): Pro-Life

    “I’m unapologetically pro-life,” “I believe in the importance of the crisis pregnancy care network, which I’m going to help fund,”

    Source: Where Ohio's candidates for governor stand on abortion, 10/28/2026
    Amy Acton (D): Pro-Abortion

    "I will protect reproductive freedom so that every woman can access safe, legal abortion..."

    Source: Acton's own campaign website

    Guns / Second Amendment

    Vivek Ramaswamy (R): Pro 2nd Amendment

    "I will be the most pro-Constitution, pro-freedom, pro-2A governor this state has ever had,"

    Source: Ramaswamy v. Acton: Choice is clear, 09/10/2026
    Amy Acton (D): Pro Gun-control

    Mostly silent on the topic, removed info from campaign site. Supports stronger background checks and red-flag laws.

    Source: Ramaswamy v. Acton: Choice is clear, 09/10/2026

    Medical Freedom

    Vivek Ramaswamy (R): Mostly Pro-freedom

    Showed some support for lockdowns. Has since strongly opposed. “No mask mandates,... No vaccine mandates. No lockdown ever again.”

    Source: 2024 Candidates Push Back Against Future COVID-19 Lockdowns, Mandates
    Amy Acton (D): Strongly Pro-Lockdown

    As Ohio Department of Health Director in 2020, Acton strongly advocated for strong lockdown rules.

    Source: Can 'Ohio's Anthony Fauci' Beat Vivek Ramaswamy?

    Education / school choice

    Vivek Ramaswamy (R): Pro School-choice

    Strongly supports giving parents right to choose where to send their children.

    Source: Ramaswamy gives his vision for education at Hilliard event, 08/21/2025
    Amy Acton (D): Anti School-choice

    Plans to restrict tax dollars from private or homeschooling. "Public money should be for public schools."

    Source: Acton's own campaign website (education)

    U.S. Senate: Jon Husted (R) vs. Sherrod Brown (D)

    IssueJon Husted (R)Sherrod Brown (D)

    Election integrity

    Jon Husted (R): Pro Voter-ID

    Offered a Senate amendment requiring photo ID to vote.

    Source: Senate rejects Husted photo-ID amendment, 03/26/2026
    Sherrod Brown (D): Anti Voter-ID

    Called the photo-ID requirement an "unnecessary barrier." "It's wrong that people are trying to sow distrust in our election systems."

    Source: Husted push for federal voter ID fails; Brown calls it unnecessary, 03/24/2026

    Immigration / border security

    Jon Husted (R): Pro Border-security

    Given a score of 100% on border security by Turning Point Action.

    Source: Turning Point Action scorecard: Jon Husted
    Sherrod Brown (D): Anti Border-enforcement

    Voted against restarting border wall construction, hiring more Border Patrol agents, and ending catch and release.

    Source: Sherrod Brown Won't Answer if ICE Should be Abolished, 08/28/2026

    NRA grade A: "Solidly pro-gun candidate."

    Sherrod Brown (D): Pro Gun-control

    NRA grade F: "True enemy of gun owners' rights."

    Source: NRA-PVF Ohio grades, 2026

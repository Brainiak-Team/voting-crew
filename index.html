    Your privacy

    Short version: the names you type stay on your device. This site has no accounts, no cookies, no ads and no tracking, and it never sends your list anywhere.

    The names on your list

    • Names are saved only in this browser, on this device, in its built-in "local storage". They are never uploaded. There is no copy on any server, so no one elsewhere, including the people who run this site, can see them.
    • The page's security settings let it connect only to this website, and the one request it makes (for the race odds) carries no names.
    • Your list does not follow you to other devices or browsers. Anyone who uses this browser on this device can open the page and see it.
    • To erase a name, delete its text. To erase everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings. In a private or incognito window, the list is erased when the window closes. Some browsers, Safari included, may clear it on their own if you have not visited the site in about a week.
    • Browser extensions you have installed can read the pages you visit, this one included, as on any website.
    • "Print a copy!" uses your own browser's print window. Nothing is sent to us.

    What this site does not do

    • No accounts, sign-ups, emails or phone numbers.
    • No cookies, analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or social media buttons.
    • No outside scripts, fonts or images. Everything comes from this one site.
    • No access to your camera, microphone or location. The site blocks itself from asking.
    • When you follow a link away from this site, your browser is told not to reveal that you came from here.

    The race odds

    The odds come from Kalshi, a prediction market. This website's own server asks Kalshi for them and reuses each answer for every visitor for about 10 minutes. Your browser never contacts Kalshi, and Kalshi never learns who you are or that you visited.

    What the web host sees

    This site is hosted by Vercel. As with any website, loading the page means your browser connects to Vercel's servers, which receive basic technical details: your IP address (a rough indication of location), your browser and device type, the time, and the page requested. Vercel uses this to deliver the site and protect it from attacks, and keeps it under its own privacy policy. Vercel's dashboard also shows the site's operators recent request records containing these details. They never include your list, and this site adds no analytics or tracking of its own. The connection is encrypted (HTTPS).

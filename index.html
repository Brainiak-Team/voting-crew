My Voting Crew

Pick up to five friends you'll take to the polls this election. Keep the list handy, and check them off as you go.

Private by design. Your list is saved only in this browser on this device. It is never sent to a server, and there are no accounts, ads, or trackers.

    Ohio race odds

    What traders on a prediction market currently think. It's a market price, not a poll, and not a result.

    Before you go

    • Check your registration, polling place, and hours with your official state or local election office.
    • Ask your friends first. A first name or nickname is enough for your list.
    • Offer rides, share the time to go, and bring any ID your state asks for.

    About your privacy

    Names are stored in your browser's local storage on this device only. Clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or pressing "Erase my list" removes them. Other devices and other browsers will not see your list.

    A community project. This page does not collect or share any information.
